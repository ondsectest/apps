Delete account says nothing about an active Pro plan

IssueSettings, AccountDesktop app v1.6.1337, MacBook Pro

Steps to reproduce

  1. Sign in with an account on an active Pro plan.
  2. Open Settings, then Account, and click Delete account.
  3. Read the confirmation dialog.

What happens

  • The dialog only says it will delete data, memory and dictionary, locally and on the servers.
  • It says nothing about the Pro plan. Wispr Flow's help docs say that deleting an account with an active subscription cancels it immediately with no refund, not even a prorated one, and that referral credits are permanently deleted.
  • A paying user can lose the rest of their plan without being told. As a Pro user, the tester felt anxious and unsure whether Pro would be lost.

What was expected

  • When the account has an active paid plan, the dialog says the plan is cancelled immediately with no refund, and that referral credits are lost.
  • It offers Manage plan and Contact support, so people can get a refund first or change their mind.

Warn about the Pro plan before deleting

ImprovementSettings, AccountDesktop app, MacBook Pro

What changes

  • For accounts with an active paid plan, an amber warning appears in the dialog: the plan is cancelled right away, there is no refund, and referral credits are deleted.
  • Manage plan and Contact support buttons sit in the warning, so people can request a refund first.
  • A tick box, "I understand my Pro plan will be cancelled immediately with no refund", must be ticked as well as typing the email before Delete account turns on.
  • Accounts on the free plan see the dialog as it is today.

Why it is better

  • No paying user loses money by surprise.
  • It matches what the help docs already say, inside the product, at the moment it matters.
  • It lowers support load from refund requests after accidental deletions.
