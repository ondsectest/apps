No way to search history or jump to a date

IssueHome, transcript historyVivo 60e, Android

Steps to reproduce

  1. Open Home, which lists every transcript grouped by day.
  2. Try to find something said a few days or weeks ago.

What happens

  • There is no search and no way to jump to a date.
  • The only way back is to scroll through every transcript, past the stats cards, day by day.
  • With heavy use, that can mean hundreds of transcripts.

Why it matters

  • People dictate messages, notes and drafts they later need again: an address, a figure, a reply they sent.
  • The history is already stored; it just cannot be found.
  • The Wispr Flow help docs describe search for the desktop Hub, but on the desktop app tested, no search was found either.

Search transcripts by words and dates

ImprovementHome, transcript historyVivo 60e, Android

What changes

  • A search icon sits at the top right of Home, where there is free space.
  • Tapping it opens a search box with the cursor ready. Results update as you type, matching words are highlighted, and the day headings stay so you know when it was said.
  • A calendar button opens a month view with From and To. Days with transcripts have a dot.
  • Tap one day for From and another for To to search a date range. If only From is chosen, results are for that one day only.
  • Search and dates work together, for example "invoice" from 20 to 25 October. The dates show as a chip with an × to clear them.
  • Clear messages when nothing matches. Cancel returns to the normal Home.

Why it is better

  • Find an old dictation in seconds instead of scrolling.
  • Matches how people remember things: by a word or by roughly when.
  • Brings Android in line with what the docs describe for desktop.
