A dictation with no speech is saved as an empty transcript

BugDictation, Home historyVivo 60e, Android

Steps to reproduce

  1. Start a dictation: tap the Flow Bubble on Android (a double press of fn on desktop behaves the same).
  2. Do not speak. Wait 3 to 5 seconds.
  3. Tap the check mark to finish.
  4. Open Wispr Flow and look at Home.

What happens

  • Nothing is typed, which is correct.
  • But Home shows a new transcript card with only a time, Copy, Report and more. There is no text in it.
  • Repeating this fills the history with blank cards.
  • Copy on a blank card copies nothing.

What was expected

  • When no speech is detected, nothing is saved to history.
  • A short message says "No speech detected", so the user knows why nothing was typed.

No speech, no transcript

Proposed fixDictation, Home history

What changes

  • When the check mark is tapped and no speech was detected, Wispr Flow shows "No speech detected. Nothing was typed, and nothing was saved to your history."
  • No transcript is added to Home. History stays clean.
  • Optionally, if blank transcripts already exist, they are hidden or cleaned up.

Why it is better

  • History only holds things that were actually said.
  • Clear feedback explains why nothing was typed, instead of leaving the user guessing.
  • No empty cards with Copy and Report buttons that do nothing useful.
