Play Store listing: the Terms of Service link includes the full stop and opens a 404

BugGoogle Play Store, app detailsWispr Flow 2.8.3, Android, Vivo 60e

Steps to reproduce

  1. Open the Google Play Store app on the phone and open Wispr Flow: AI Voice-to-Text (installed, version 2.8.3).
  2. Tap About this app to open the Details page.
  3. Scroll to Privacy & Security.
  4. Tap the link https://wisprflow.ai/terms-of-service. It opens in the browser.

What happens

  • The sentence ends with a full stop right after the URL, and Google Play makes the full stop part of the link.
  • The link opens https://wisprflow.ai/terms-of-service. with the dot, which is a 404 Page not found.
  • People checking the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy before or after installing cannot reach them from the Play Store listing.

What was expected

  • The link opens https://wisprflow.ai/terms-of-service, the real Terms of Service page.

Remove the dot after the link

Proposed fixGoogle Play Store, app details

The fix

  • In the Play Console, remove the full stop after the URL in the app's full description, so the sentence ends with https://wisprflow.ai/terms-of-service.
  • Tapping the link now opens the real Terms of Service page.

Also worth checking

  • Other links in the Play Store and App Store listings, and in the app, that are followed by punctuation, such as a full stop, comma or closing bracket.
  • The website could also redirect /terms-of-service. to /terms-of-service, so old copies of the text still work.
